How a crew member is scored

Weights are shown as shares of 100%. Workload balance = 1 − (days assigned in the 90 days either side of the mission start ÷ 180). "Meets the bar" gives 60% for the minimum level and 10 more for each level above it. Rest = days since their last mission ended, capped at 30, ÷ 30; someone who has never flown counts as fully rested.
